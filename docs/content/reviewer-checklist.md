# Lesson and media reviewer checklist

Complete one checklist per lesson version. Keep the signed-off record with the content source materials; do not place student personal information in lesson text or media.

## Curriculum and science

- [ ] Subject, level, course, term and topic match an identified curriculum source.
- [ ] Source/exam-body mapping is recorded when the product owner has confirmed the authority.
- [ ] Scientific statements, units, procedure and expected outcomes were checked by a qualified subject reviewer.
- [ ] Language is suitable for the intended secondary learner and avoids shaming or unsafe imitation.

## Safety and accessibility

- [ ] Safety notes identify relevant hazards and safe handling; no unsafe real-world instructions are implied by a simulation.
- [ ] Every audio item has an accurate transcript and synchronized/usable captions.
- [ ] Video has an accessible text equivalent; images have meaningful alt text in the rendered lesson.
- [ ] Content works with keyboard navigation, screen readers, reduced motion and a narrow mobile viewport.
- [ ] Media is optional to understanding where data cost or connectivity may prevent playback.

## Rights and technical checks

- [ ] Every asset has a recorded creator/source, attribution, license, permission evidence and checksum in `asset-register.csv`.
- [ ] The license permits this use and distribution in the product's intended territories.
- [ ] MIME type, dimensions/duration, size and file integrity were checked; no executable or active content is included.
- [ ] Text and assets contain no credentials, private information or unreviewed user-generated content.
- [ ] Version/changelog is accurate; publication is approved by a reviewer other than the requester and by a second staff member.
